Our Story

We Started with Design. We Grew into Growth.

Superknown was born in 2021, at a time when businesses around the world were rebuilding after the pandemic. What started as a creative studio has since evolved into a full-service creative and marketing agency, helping brands build stronger identities, tell better stories, and achieve measurable growth.

Behind Superknown is Ronnie Datta, a creative professional with over 12 years of experience in the freelance industry. Long before Superknown was established, Ronnie collaborated with businesses of every scale—from local startups to international brands—creating visual identities, brand systems, advertising campaigns, photography, films, and digital experiences.

As the market evolved, so did client expectations. Businesses no longer needed just good design—they needed strategy, marketing, technology, and measurable business outcomes. That shift transformed Superknown.
