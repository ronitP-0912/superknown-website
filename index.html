Our Story

Built on 12 Years of Creative Passion

Ronnie Datta began his creative journey over twelve years ago as an independent freelancer, helping businesses communicate through thoughtful branding, visual storytelling, and design. As his experience and client base grew, so did the ambition to build a creative studio that offered more than isolated services.

Superknown was founded to bring together designers, editors, strategists, and marketers into one collaborative agency capable of delivering complete creative solutions across hospitality, education, retail, lifestyle, and emerging businesses.
